Concept

A full-screen field of dots that ripples outward from the mouse, like
dropping a stone into water. Hundreds of identical circles follow the
same simple rule, but because each one sits at a different distance
from the cursor, together they form moving rings of color.

How to interact

- Move your mouse across the screen: the ripples follow the cursor
- Keep the mouse still to watch the waves pulse in place
- Resize the window: the pattern fills the new size

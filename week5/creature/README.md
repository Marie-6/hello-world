Concept

Pocket Critter is a tiny pixel animal that lives in a meadow with a leaf
bed and an apple tree. The first time you visit, there is only an egg in
the grass. Tap it until it hatches, find out what animal it is, and give
it a name. It remembers you (its name, its outfit and how big it has
grown) the next time you come back.

Every egg is one of six species: kitten, bunny, puppy, bear cub, fox or
panda. The pattern on the egg is a hint (stripes, spots or patches). When
you start over with a new egg, it is always a species you haven't raised
yet, until you've met all six.

How to interact

- Tap the egg: it cracks, hatches, and asks for a name
- FEED: an apple drops from the tree and it trots over to eat it
- Click the grass: drop a strawberry
- SLEEP: it walks to its leaf bed, night falls, stars and fireflies come out
- Wiggle the mouse on it: you pet it, hearts float up and its tail wags
- Click it: it hops and says hi
- CLOSET: dress it up with hats (bow, party hat, beanie, sun hat, frog hat,
  crown) and clothes (scarf, sweater, overalls, tutu). Some only unlock
  once it has grown big enough
- When it's happy (fed, rested and petted), it follows your mouse around.
  When it's hungry, sleepy or lonely, it wanders, looks worried and tells
  you so

Growing up

Every meal and every pet fills the GROW bar. It goes from baby → little →
big → grown. Its head and body get bigger, its ears and tail grow longer
(the fox's tail gets really fluffy, and a grown bunny gets one floppy ear),
little arms appear, its eyes get bigger, and the kitten grows whiskers.
